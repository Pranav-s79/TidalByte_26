# Sponsor prize lenses — don't let these distort the problem

## NVIDIA — Nemotron agent doing real work
**User-provided criterion:** best use of NVIDIA Nemotron; the agent should be doing real work.

An aligned concept can answer:
1. **Task owner:** who needs a job finished?
2. **Trigger/input:** what activates the task, and what information is available?
3. **Decision:** what uncertain/reasoning-heavy step does **Nemotron** perform (not merely a brand mention)?
4. **Work completed:** what artifact, verified status, prepared action, organized case, drafted request, or approved change results?
5. **Verification:** how could a person tell the task was done correctly?
6. **Boundaries:** what needs consent, review, or professional escalation?

Avoid 'a chatbot that tells people what to do'. Prefer 'an agent that handles a bounded workflow and produces a checkable result'. A drafted work order, triaged request, completed cross-source comparison, or verified packet can count as meaningful work **conceptually**; whether specific integration is practical belongs outside this folder.

Official NVIDIA material describes Nemotron for reasoning, tool calling, multimodal understanding, retrieval, and agent workflows. That supports concept exploration but DOES NOT confirm hackathon-specific APIs, minimum usage, model versions, or winning formula. Source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-data-science/foundation-models/nemotron/

## Adobe — best design
**User-provided criterion:** best design. No confirmed requirement to use Adobe products.

An aligned concept can answer:
1. What information is currently hard to understand, navigate, or trust?
2. What **design transformation** makes the experience dramatically clearer?
3. What would an observer see before and after?
4. How does layout, visual hierarchy, accessibility, or storytelling reduce effort?
5. Is the attractive presentation part of solving the problem, rather than superficial decoration?

Good **idea-level** experiences: visual comparisons, maps, timelines, accessible storytelling, meaningful status, uncertainty explanations, step-by-step guidance, coordinated forms, understandable data narratives. Avoid inventing a requirement for Firefly/Express/Photoshop.

Adobe's *separate* MAX Design Awards (NOT this hackathon) publicly emphasize originality, visual impact, cohesion, usability, and functionality; use as generic design inspiration only, never as the hackathon's official rubric. Source: https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2026/06/18/adobe-max-2026-creativity-awards-here-are-categories-and-how-to-submit
