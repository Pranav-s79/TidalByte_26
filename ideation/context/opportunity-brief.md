# What we want to ideate — direction, not implementation

## North-star idea
A project where AI/ML addresses a **specific and genuine** pain point in a way that crosses disciplines, changes an outcome, feels distinct from the usual chatbot, and can be explained in a visually clear pitch. Sponsor prizes are optional routes, not reasons to invent a problem.

## Preferred idea traits (not requirements)
1. Clear **human beneficiary**: e.g., school staff, community volunteers, tenants, small manufacturers, caregivers, mobility-limited residents, local governments, farmers, or nonprofit coordinators.
2. Concrete **job to be done**: a routine that currently consumes attention, is confusing, falls between organizations, or is inaccessible.
3. **Cross-disciplinary friction**: information or decisions cross at least two domains (e.g., environment + city operations; accessibility + urban mobility; public health administration + language access; agriculture + logistics; education + workforce training; disaster preparedness + community support).
4. Genuine **AI role**: interpret complex inputs, reconcile conflicting records, detect meaningful patterns, coordinate follow-up, or personalize a decision sequence. Reject AI that merely rephrases text.
5. Specific **visible result**: comparison, map, annotated document, saved task, case resolution, clear user-facing timeline, or changed status. These are conceptual, not implementation commitments.
6. **Responsible agency**: for high-impact or private decisions, the agent may prepare/check/route work but the user approves consequential action.
7. One-sentence pitch and vivid 45–60 second **before → agent action/design transformation → after** demonstration story.

## Ten exploration territories (SEEDS ONLY — do not submit these as finished ideas)
- Accessibility × public spaces: making an inaccessible process understandable and navigable.
- Education × local workforce: tracking opportunities or supporting real student workflows without becoming a homework chatbot.
- Environment × city operations: turning scattered reports into prioritized community action.
- Agriculture × logistics: helping small operators coordinate waste, inventory, timing, or resource sharing.
- Public services × language access: making confusing, multilingual administrative tasks less error-prone.
- Community health administration × transportation: assisting with non-clinical scheduling and follow-up, not diagnosis.
- Small business × regulation: clarifying ordinary administrative paperwork with human oversight.
- Disaster preparedness × neighborhood coordination: preparing actionable, verifiable checklists without claiming forecasts.
- Manufacturing × safety training: making complicated procedures easier to understand without replacing certified safety processes.
- Civic engagement × design: making hard-to-read local information approachable and actionable.

## Banned easy defaults unless exceptional
- Generic AI study buddy; PDF chatbot; summarizer; travel planner; vague mental-health chatbot; recipe recommender; dashboard with no useful behavior; 'AI for sustainability' without a specific user/problem; generic resume optimizer.
- If an idea resembles these, articulate the substantive new work performed and a distinct unmet need or discard it.

## Creativity pressure test
Every discovery round must deliberately generate: (a) 2 NVIDIA-action candidates, (b) 2 Adobe-design-led candidates, (c) 2 cross-disciplinary wildcards, and (d) 2 ideas selected purely for user impact. Ideas may overlap categories, but at least 5 **different sectors** must appear across the 8-card QUICK set, where evidence supports it. This is a diversity *target for exploration*, not an official rubric interpretation.

## Do not drift into engineering
No stack/framework lists; no choosing exact Nemotron model versions; no API account steps; no integration plans; no implementation testing; no effort estimates. Briefly state the AI's role in plain English and what users experience.
