# Rubric interpretation — IDEATION ONLY

**Not official weighting.** The prompt supplies names but no definitions or percentages. Use a 1–5 scale for each, equal-weight average as a *working comparison*, and record uncertainty. Re-run against official rules if available.

| Criterion | 1 = weak | 3 = moderate | 5 = strong | What an idea card must explain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Technicality | AI is cosmetic | AI performs a useful classification/reasoning step | AI is necessary to solve an interesting multi-step, multimodal, or decision-heavy problem | What does AI actually do that a basic form/search cannot? |
| Ingenuity | Generic wrapper, duplicated pitch | Some original focus or recombination | Distinctive insight/approach for an overlooked pain point | Why is it not just an existing app? |
| Diversity* | Relevant to one narrow silo | Connects two disciplines | Meaningfully bridges 3+ perspectives or stakeholders | Which disciplines benefit, and how do they interact? |
| Applicability | No identifiable user or outcome | Plausible use case with unknown need | Clear user, trigger, and outcome tied to credible pain | Who needs this and what changes for them? |
| Looks / pitchability | Hard to explain or invisible effect | Understandable story but limited visual contrast | Compelling before/after; natural one-minute walkthrough | What would judges see, and what is the wow moment? |

`*` Provisional interpretation of **Diversity** as cross-disciplinary reach, reflecting the event prompt. Verify with official event rubric; never silently claim this definition is official.

## Cross-cutting review (not extra rubric dimensions)
- Evidence quality: verified / plausible / speculative.
- User-centered risks: privacy, access, safety, exclusion, trust. Note if relevant, but avoid engineering proposals.
- Sponsor alignment: evaluate separately for NVIDIA, Adobe, both (subject to eligibility), or general.
- Never add fictional quantitative impact, API access, or judge preferences.
- Avoid false precision. Tie every rating to one short sentence, especially a 4 or 5.

## Calculation
`overall = (technicality + ingenuity + diversity + applicability + looks) / 5` (range 1–5) **only while official weights unknown**. Sponsor fit tags are not bonus points. Break ties by strength of sourced problem and idea differentiation rather than decimals.
