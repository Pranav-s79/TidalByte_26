# Adobe track: best design

**Known from user:** Best design. No other obligations are confirmed.

**Idea-screen questions:**
- Is design central to improving a real user outcome?
- Does it make difficult information understandable at a glance?
- Is the user journey memorable, intuitive, and accessible?
- Does the visual presentation make the cross-disciplinary impact obvious?
- Can a judge perceive the difference in under a minute?

**Strong design spaces:** maps, data narratives, transparent decisions, comparison views, accessibility, status storytelling, guided decision experiences.

**Not good enough:** stylish dashboard unrelated to the underlying problem; branding alone; assuming Adobe tool use is mandatory without evidence.

**UNKNOWN:** official Adobe tools or assets requirement, judging rules, eligible submission types.
