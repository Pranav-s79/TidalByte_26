# Discovery contract

Collect **useful evidence**, not exhaustive reading. Prefer primary official sponsor sources for capabilities and original community conversations for pain points. Past winners are inspiration and originality checks, not templates to copy.

Every source needs a stable `SRC-###` entry in `sources.md` with title, URL, origin, access date, and concise takeaway. Every note must separate **what the source says** from **our hypothesis**. A single Reddit comment does not prove a widespread need.

Scouts keep their own files in the appropriate directory; coordinator merges IDs and removes duplicates. Limit QUICK mode to ~3 unique sources per scout. Do not search dozens of similar pages to fill a quota.
