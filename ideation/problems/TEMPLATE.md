# P-### — short problem label

- **Audience:** who experiences this, specifically?
- **Situation/trigger:** when does it happen?
- **Pain:** what breaks, delays, or confuses?
- **Current workaround:** what do users do today?
- **Cross-disciplinary connection:** domain A × domain B and the friction between them.
- **Evidence:** `SRC-###` + URL or `HYPOTHESIS — needs evidence`.
- **Confidence:** evidenced / plausible / speculative.
