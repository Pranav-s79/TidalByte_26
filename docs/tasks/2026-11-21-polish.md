## Pri: Flood math (prediction + router)

The spec for tasks 1 and 2 is [`docs/routing-and-prediction.md`](../routing-and-prediction.md), "The math".

1. **Prediction**, `flood/predict.py`. A Gaussian process per camera over time (add `scikit-learn` to `requirements.txt`). It returns the mean, σ and rate of rise at a given time, never more than 60 min ahead, and None when there are fewer than 4 readings in the last 90 min. *Done when:* the predict tests listed in the spec pass.
2. **Least-deep router**, `flood/route.py`:
   - Planning depth per segment, as in the spec: camera segments use μ + σ at arrival time (from `predict.py`); estimated segments use the fill's high run; replay uses `max_depth_m`. Unknown segments stay routable but are counted as unobserved.
   - Drop segments deeper than the person-set limit, then try depth caps in steps (0, 5, 10 cm …). The first cap that connects start to end wins, and networkx finds the shortest path under that cap.
   - Return 2–3 options, each with distance, deepest point and unobserved distance.
   *Done when:* toy-graph tests pass, covering: a shallower but longer route beating a deeper short one, no route under the limit returning "no route within your limit", and unobserved distance counted.
3. **Run the router on the replay data**, so routing is demoable today. Write `web/data/routes.json` for a fixed start/end that crosses Meyerland.
4. **Review loop:** `flood/serve.py` handles `POST /review {id, status}`, which updates the observations and reruns `build_outputs`. Test: confirming a reading raises `n_confirmed_photos`.
5. **Clean up slivers.** Drop water regions under ~20 cells and simplify polygons. Target: files under half their current size, tests green.
